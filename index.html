A MESSAGE FROM MY HEART

One Last
Message

I don't know if you'll ever see this, but there are a few things I wanted to say.

To someone who still matters to me ♡

Hey...

I know things between us aren't the same anymore. And I know I can't change the past.

There are things I wish I had understood earlier. There are mistakes I wish I could undo. If I had understood what you needed from me, maybe I could have done things differently.

I'm not writing this to blame you or make you feel guilty. I just wanted you to know that what we had meant a lot to me.

I still care about you. And a part of me still hopes that someday, when everything feels a little calmer, we might be able to talk again.

If you ever want to give us one more chance, I'd be grateful.

But if you don't, I'll respect your decision too. I don't want my feelings to become a burden for you.

Take care of yourself.

— Pintu
